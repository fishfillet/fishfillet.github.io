What is Catlico?

Catlico is an Indonesian cat-centric brand — a destination for cat parents who want products that genuinely improve the lives of their cats, not just products that tick a box. We believe cats and cat parents deserve better: better materials, better care, better thought.

Every product we make starts with a single question: is this truly good for the cat? From there, we look at the cat parent's experience — because a product that's hard to use doesn't get used. Our approach is whole-cat-care: we think holistically about what makes a cat's life great, and design from there.

We're based in Indonesia and proud of it. Our first product, Better Bentonite Cat Litter, was born from the frustration of not being able to find a truly natural, unscented, high-quality clumping litter for our own cats. So we made one.

Why Catlico?

Natural & Safe

All Catlico products are made without harmful chemicals, artificial fragrances, or synthetic dyes. What's good for your cat is good for your home — and we never compromise on that.

Cat-Centric

We design every product around the cat's needs first. Cats have sensitive noses, delicate paws, and strong instincts. Our products respect all of that — because that's what it means to be cat-centric.

Thoughtful Design

From the formula to the packaging to the way it pours — every detail is considered. Good design makes cat care feel effortless, and that's exactly what we aim for with every Catlico product.
