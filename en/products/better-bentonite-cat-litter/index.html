Why Fragrance-Free?

Cats have up to 200 million odor receptors — roughly 40 times more than humans. What smells mild to us can be overwhelming and even distressing for a cat. Artificial fragrances in cat litter are a common but hidden cause of litter box avoidance in cats.

When a cat walks through scented litter, the fragrance chemicals coat their paws and then enter their body when they groom themselves. Cats are obligate carnivores with limited ability to metabolize certain synthetic compounds — meaning some fragrance additives that are harmless to us can accumulate in their system over time.

Better Bentonite Cat Litter contains absolutely no fragrances, perfumes, or masking agents. The odor control comes entirely from the natural absorptive properties of high-grade bentonite clay — the way nature intended.

Your cat will notice the difference. And so will you — because a cat that feels comfortable in their litter box is a cat that uses it consistently.

How to Use

  1. 1
    Fill the litter box to 7–10 cm depth

    Pour Better Bentonite Cat Litter into a clean litter box to a depth of 7 to 10 centimetres. A deeper layer gives your cat room to dig and improves clump formation.

  2. 2
    Scoop clumps daily

    Use a slotted scoop to remove solid clumps each day. Daily scooping is the single most effective thing you can do to maintain freshness and extend the litter's lifespan.

  3. 3
    Top up as needed

    After scooping, add a little fresh litter to maintain the ideal 7–10 cm depth. This keeps the litter box comfortable and effective between full changes.

  4. 4
    Replace fully every 1–2 weeks

    Empty the litter box completely, wash and dry it, then refill with fresh litter. Depending on how many cats you have, a full change every 1 to 2 weeks keeps things consistently fresh.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Better Bentonite Cat Litter fragrance-free?

Yes. Better Bentonite Cat Litter contains no fragrances, perfumes, or artificial scents of any kind. It is completely unscented — safe for cats with sensitive noses and respiratory systems. Odor control is achieved naturally through the absorptive properties of high-grade bentonite clay.

Does it work with automatic litter boxes?

Yes. Better Bentonite Cat Litter is fully compatible with automatic and self-cleaning litter boxes, including Litter Robot, PETKIT, PetSafe ScoopFree, and Whisker models. The firm, fast clumps do not crumble in raking mechanisms, keeping your auto box running smoothly.

Is it safe for kittens?

Yes. Made from 100% natural bentonite clay with no chemicals, fragrances, or dyes, Better Bentonite Cat Litter is safe for cats of all ages including kittens. As with all clumping litters, we recommend supervising very young kittens (under 4 weeks) until they learn not to eat the litter.

Better Bentonite Cat Litter provides up to 30 days of odor control with proper daily scooping. The natural bentonite clay binds and locks odor molecules on contact — keeping your home fresh without artificial masking agents.

How is the litter sourced?

The bentonite clay used in Better Bentonite Cat Litter is sourced from the USA — one of the world's leading sources of high-quality sodium bentonite. We use the highest grade available to ensure optimal clumping performance and purity.

Can it be flushed down the toilet?

No. We do not recommend flushing any clumping bentonite litter down the toilet, as it can expand and cause blockages in plumbing systems. Please dispose of used litter in regular household waste (sealed bag recommended).
